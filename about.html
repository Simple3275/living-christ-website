Who We Are

Our Vision & Mission

Raising a praying generation, restoring spiritual foundations, and proclaiming the uncompromised Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To build an altar of prayer and worship where lives are transformed by the divine power of God, believers are established in righteous living, and souls are won for Christ.

Our Mission

Preaching sound Biblical truth, conducting intensive spiritual warfare, nurturing disciples through Bible study, and providing physical and spiritual care to our community.

What We Believe

1. The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God.

2. The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

3. Salvation

Salvation is received through faith in Jesus Christ, His shed blood, death, and resurrection.

4. Prayer & Deliverance

We believe in the present manifestation of the Holy Spirit's power, divine healing, and deliverance through prayer.

Living Christ Christian Power Ministry
